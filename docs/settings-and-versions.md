# Settings and SCRCPY Versions

Open **Settings** from the information panel on the Home screen.

## Preferences

- **Language**: choose English, Italian, or Japanese.
- **Do not check for updates**: skip the SCRCPY+ release check shown on Home. This does not disable SCRCPY version management.
- **Remember SCRCPY config**: restore the saved launch options when the app opens again.

## SCRCPY and ADB executables

The executable fields let you choose SCRCPY and ADB files on your computer. When no custom path is selected, SCRCPY+ uses its platform defaults. Custom paths are saved for later launches. Make sure selected files are the intended executables and are compatible with your platform.

If ADB is missing, the Home screen offers a download. The **SCRCPY versions** page, linked from Settings, lists stable SCRCPY releases and indicates when a build is not available for your platform. Selecting **Download & use** downloads a compatible release bundle and makes its SCRCPY and ADB executables the active pair.

The SCRCPY+ update check and the SCRCPY version manager are separate: the former opens the SCRCPY+ release page when an update is available; the latter installs a SCRCPY release bundle.
